# Noctis v1.7.2 — Windows 7 SP1 & Bit-Perfect Lossless Edition

This cumulative release brings upstream **Noctis v1.7.0**, **v1.7.1**, and **v1.7.2** into the dedicated **Windows 7 SP1 (x64) & Bit-Perfect Lossless Edition** (superseding `v1.6.9.4`), delivering major audio engine upgrades, Masonry & Genre views, Skip Silence, playlist folders, custom lyrics renderers, and Windows 7 / Avalonia 11 rendering fixes.

---

### 🌟 Highlights Across v1.7.0 – v1.7.2

#### 🔊 Audio Engine, Gapless & Playback Upgrades
- **Skip Silence**: Automatically starts songs at their first audible sound and ends after their last, powered by built-in LibVLC scanning with an adjustable threshold under **Settings → Playback**.
- **Self-Resampling Gapless Engine & File Pre-Warming**: Sample-rate conversion moved directly into the gapless engine with background pre-reading (`PlaybackFileWarmer`) for the active and staged next track—eliminating seek stalls on mechanical HDDs and ensuring sample-exact transitions across mixed sample rates, DSD, Vorbis, HE-AAC, and very short tracks.
- **Native Stop Button & Exclusive Mode Hardware Release (Upstreamed & Preserved)**: Seamless migration of legacy Win7 Stop button settings (`PlaybackBarShowStopButton` / `PlaybackBarStopButtonExclusiveOnly`) into upstream settings keys, while continuing to dispose `_exclusiveOut` on Stop to immediately release the DAC hardware lock for other apps.
- **Bit-Perfect & Codec Updates**: Upgraded bundled **LibVLC to 3.0.24** (`LibVLCSharp 3.10.2`), added `.alac` and `.aifc` library support, fixed release date saving across formats, and fixed AIFF tag header sizes.
- **Sleep Timer & Audiobooks**: Releases the Exclusive Mode audio device when the sleep timer halts playback; added **Chapters** button on the Player Island for audiobooks.

#### 🎨 Library, Views & Customization
- **Masonry Layout, Song Counts & Genres Page**: Added Masonry grid layout across Albums, Genre, Artist, and Home views; added song counts on album cards, multi-value **Genre Tag Separators**, clickable genre/artist/song links, and an optional **Genres** page.
- **Playlist Folders & Multi-Playlist Adding**: Organize playlists into folders (**New Folder**, **Move to Folder**, **Remove Empty Folders**, Top/Bottom position), add tracks to multiple playlists at once, and view/edit full-size playlist covers.
- **Album & Artist Enhancements**: Added **Keep Shuffle On**, **Song Version** tags (searched and displayed in *Other Versions*), **Show Star Ratings** toggle, clickable **Album song-row hearts**, **Full Artwork** mini-player mode, carousel toggles on Singles/EPs & Live Albums, cover-hover zoom & glow, and **Move to Album** for songs.
- **Window & UI Polish**: Added **Tint window with accent colour**, **Hide sidebar until hover**, **Playing from** source display in Queue/Player Island, optional volume slider & time toggle on the Player Island, Shift+click & rubber-band selection, and remembered monitor placement for maximized windows.

#### 📝 Lyrics, Plugins & Account Sync
- **Lyrics Studio & Syncing**: Added `0.5×`–`1.5×` playback speed, disc scratching on the lyrics page and Sleeve mini-player, **ELRC / LRC** file output selector, unlimited-length lyrics windowing, **Add/Edit Lyrics** on the lyrics page, **Auto-return to current line**, whole-library background lyrics search (with optional `lrcmux` source), and `Word Light` sample **LyricsRenderer** plugin API.
- **Last.fm & Multi-Device Sync**: Added Last.fm as a **Statistics** source and **Other computers** play/rating/favourite sync under **Settings → Account & Devices**.

### 🛡️ Windows 7 SP1 x64 & .NET 6 / Avalonia 11 Fixes
- **SkiaSharp Offscreen `BlurredBackdrop` (Settings Black Screen Fix)**: Replaced Avalonia 12's hidden visual-tree opacity stand-in with offscreen SkiaSharp pixel blurring (`SKImageFilter.CreateBlur`), fixing the solid-black Settings backdrop on Avalonia 11 while preserving smooth stand-in scrolling.
- **Avalonia 11 & .NET 6 Compatibility**:
  - Updated `KaraokeWordText` to Avalonia 11 `Offset` / `LayoutHelper.RoundLayoutThickness`.
  - Fixed `BlurredBackdrop`, `CachedImage` (`Dispatcher.UIThread.Post`), `KeyboardFocusScroll` (`GotFocusEventArgs`), `MoveToAlbumDialog` (`SystemDecorations`), `CommandPaletteDialog`, and `SettingsView` (`Watermark`).
  - Added `.NET 6` polyfills and fallbacks for `JsonNode.DeepClone`, `char.IsAsciiLetter` / `IsAsciiHexDigit`, `X509Certificate2`, and `HttpContent.LoadIntoBufferAsync`.
- **Legacy Settings Migration**: `AppSettings.OnDeserialized()` automatically migrates your existing v1.6.7–v1.6.9.4 Stop button preferences.
- **Windows 7 Foundations Retained**: Bundled `yt-dlp_win7.exe` routing for YouTube Import, ANGLE Direct3D 11 acceleration, Skia overlay tooltips, Win32 rounded MiniPlayer menu clipping, and Deezer HTTP Schannel compatibility.

### 🔄 Updating from v1.6.9 – v1.6.9.4
If you are running **Noctis v1.6.9** through **v1.6.9.4** (portable Windows 7 build):
1. Open Noctis and go to **Settings → About**.
2. Click **Check for updates** → **Download** → **Install & restart**.
3. Noctis downloads `Noctis-v1.7.2-Win7-x64.zip`, verifies `SHA256SUMS`, stages the files, replaces your portable installation in-place, and relaunches automatically with your library and settings intact.

*(If updating manually, extract `Noctis-v1.7.2-Win7-x64.zip` over your existing Noctis folder and run `Noctis.exe`.)*
