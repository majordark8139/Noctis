# Noctis v1.6.9 — Windows 7 SP1 & Bit-Perfect Lossless Edition

This release merges upstream **Noctis v1.6.9** into our dedicated Windows 7 SP1 & Bit-Perfect Lossless Edition, delivering **Multichannel Surround Sound (5.1 & 7.1)**, an extensive **Lyrics Management Suite**, **"Other Versions"** track intelligence, **Sidebar Customization**, **Offline Scrobbling Queue**, and full-audible-range spectrum visualization — all natively optimized for Windows 7 SP1 x64 and audiophile bit-perfect hardware playback.

---

### 🌟 What's New in v1.6.9

#### 🔊 Multichannel (5.1 & 7.1) Surround Sound Engine
- **Native Discrete Surround Playback**: Full discrete playback for 5.1 and 7.1 multichannel files (FLAC 5.1/7.1, DTS, AC3, surround WAV) across every speaker without downmixing.
- **Bit-Perfect WASAPI Multi-Speaker Routing**: Configures `WAVEFORMATEXTENSIBLE` channel masks to lock and send discrete surround channels directly to multi-channel DACs, surround soundcards, or HDMI AV receivers.
- **Surround Balance & Visualizer**: Balance, mono toggle, and the real-time audio visualizer now natively support multichannel streams.

#### 📝 Comprehensive Lyrics Suite & Background Engine
- **Auto-Upgrade Plain Lyrics**: Automatically scans and upgrades plain, unsynced text lyrics to time-synced word-by-word / karaoke (`.elrc` / `.lrc`) formats.
- **Album-Wide "Find Lyrics"**: Batch search and fetch lyrics for entire albums directly from the album context menu.
- **Pre-Save Review Dialog**: Inspect newly discovered lyrics side-by-side in a review window before saving or replacing files on disk.
- **Background Auto-Fetcher**: Silently fetches lyrics in the background whenever new songs are added to your library.
- **Search by Lyrics**: Search for songs by singing or typing lyric phrases directly from the search pill (**"Include Lyrics"** and **"Lyrics Only"** filters).

#### 🔀 "Other Versions" Track Intelligence
- **Alternative Track Grouping**: Automatically identifies alternate takes in your library (Live recordings, Remasters, Acoustic cuts, Instrumentals).
- **In-Menu Version Switcher**: Displays an "Other Versions" button on tracks, letting you quickly view and switch between versions with their specific album covers and release dates.

#### 🧭 Sidebar Customization & Start Page Picker
- **Reorder & Hide Tabs**: Under **Settings → General**, customize sidebar pages (Home, Songs, Albums, Artists, Playlists, Queue, Lyrics, Visualizer).
- **Default Start Page**: Choose which page Noctis opens at launch (e.g. jump straight into your Songs library or Albums instead of Home).

#### 📻 Offline Scrobble Queue & Multi-Device Isolation
- **Offline Scrobble Buffer**: When offline or if Last.fm / ListenBrainz are unreachable, plays are cached safely in a local queue and submitted automatically once connectivity returns.
- **Multi-Device Session Protection**: Separate scrobbler logins are preserved when sharing a single data folder across multiple PCs.

#### 🎛️ Queue & Playlist Ergonomics
- **Quick Add to Playlist**: Pin up to three playlists for 1-click instant track adding from songs and playback bars.
- **Move to Trash**: Permanently discard unwanted songs and their associated lyrics files directly into the Windows Recycle Bin from the player.
- **Queue Drag Insertion Indicator**: Visual line indicator showing the exact insert location when dragging files into the Queue.
- **"Show Artists as Written"**: Option to preserve exact ID3 artist tags without automatic "feat." splitting.

#### 🎨 Visualizer & UI Polish
- **Full Audible Spectrum (20 Hz – 20 kHz)**: Spectrum analyzer now spans the complete human hearing range, eliminating the flat block on deep sub-bass frequencies.
- **Edge-to-Edge Spectrum**: Smooth, edge-to-edge spectrum rendering on both the Visualizer tab and the Lyrics view.
- **Floating Glass Search Pill**: Search bar now floats above the interface without displacing page content.

#### 🪟 Windows System Fixes
- **Volume Mixer Cleanup**: Hides the silent keep-warm audio stream so the Windows Volume Mixer displays only one clean Noctis slider.
- **Windows Media Flyout (SMTC)**: Shuffle and Repeat buttons on the Windows taskbar and volume overlay now stay perfectly synchronized with app playback.
- **Tray Menu Scaling**: Fixed tray menu clipping at high interface scaling (120% – 150%) and removed black square corners with transparency disabled.

#### 🎰 Plugin API 1.7 & Song Roulette Support
- **Plugin API 1.7 (`RegisterLibraryCommand`)**: Allows plugins to register custom buttons directly into library views and menus.
- **Album Roulette 1.0.1 Support**: Fully compatible with the upgraded Album Roulette plugin featuring **Song Roulette** mode, allowing users to spin single songs with shuffle exclusion rules and action pills (Play, Play next, Add to queue, Open album) directly from the Songs top bar and Artist menus.

### 🛡️ Windows 7 SP1 & Audiophile Foundations

All existing Windows 7 Lossless Edition capabilities remain fully active:
- **Bit-Perfect WASAPI Exclusive Mode**: Direct, unadulterated PCM delivery bypassing Windows Audio Engine (`audiodg.exe`) and OS volume sliders.
- **Dynamic Sample-Rate DAC Locking**: Instant hardware clock switching across 44.1 kHz, 48 kHz, 88.2 kHz, 96 kHz, 176.4 kHz, and 192 kHz.
- **Dedicated Hardware Stop Button**: Releases exclusive DAC locks and COM endpoints immediately so other apps can play sound.
- **Native DSD Playback & Batch Transcoding**: Hardware playback of `.dsf` and `.dff` files via `DsdPcmDecoder`.
- **Aero Glass & Win32 DWM Clipping**: 36px rounded popup menus with zero white corners or rendering artifacts.
- **Bundled LibVLC 3.0.21 x64**: Complete offline codecs for FLAC, ALAC, WAV, APE, Opus, AAC, and MP3.

### 📦 Installation & Updating

#### From v1.6.8 In-App Updater
1. Open Noctis v1.6.8 and head to **Settings → About**.
2. Click **Check for updates**.
3. Noctis will download **v1.6.9** and prompt you to **Install & Restart** — updating in-place automatically!

#### Manual Portable (Windows 7 SP1 x64)
1. Download `Noctis-v1.6.9-Win7-x64.zip` from Assets below.
2. Extract into your Noctis directory, overwriting existing files.
3. Launch `Noctis.exe`.
