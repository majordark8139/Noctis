# Noctis v1.6.8 — Windows 7 SP1 & Bit-Perfect Lossless Edition

This release brings the full feature set of upstream **Noctis v1.6.8** to the Windows 7 SP1 Lossless Edition, featuring native **Direct Stream Digital (DSD)** audio playback, custom library data locations, MP3 frame damage repair with seamless decoder handover, centered no-lyrics UI, and album grouping — all fully integrated with our audiophile **Bit-Perfect WASAPI Exclusive Mode** engine.

---

### 🌟 What's New in v1.6.8

#### 🎼 Native Direct Stream Digital (DSD) Playback & Conversion
- **Native `.dsf` and `.dff` Audio Support**: Native hardware playback of high-resolution DSD64, DSD128, and DSD256 files via `DsdPcmDecoder`.
- **Bit-Perfect DAC Synchronization**: Fully routed into our WASAPI Exclusive Mode transport — your DAC clock automatically locks directly to 88.2 kHz or 96 kHz high-res PCM with zero OS resampling or mixer degradation.
- **DSD Batch Audio Converter**: Built-in conversion utility to transcode between DSD and high-resolution PCM formats directly from the app.

#### 💾 Relocatable Library Data Folder
- **Move Off System Drive**: You can now choose a custom directory for your Noctis library database, indexed metadata, and artwork cache under **Settings → Storage / Data Location**.
- **Automated Migration**: Noctis seamlessly copies and verifies library databases without losing play counts, playlists, or customized album groupings.

#### 🛡️ MP3 Damage Pre-Scan & Seamless Warm Handover
- **Frame Damage Detection**: Background frame validation scans MP3 files for corrupted or truncated MPEG frames before they reach the output buffer.
- **Zero-Gap Decoder Handover**: Automatically engages an in-memory FFmpeg warm handover right before damaged frames, eliminating audio pops, playback freezes, or playback stopping mid-track.

#### 🎨 Interface Polish & Fullscreen Enhancements
- **Centered No-Lyrics Layout**: When a track has no available lyrics or instrumental tags, the player view dynamically switches to a centered, balanced layout with expanded artwork.
- **Fullscreen Sidebar Edge Peek**: In F11 Fullscreen mode, the navigation sidebar smoothly slides out when nudging the left edge of the screen and auto-hides when moving away.
- **Albums View Carousel Style Disabling**: Albums view menu cleanly grays out carousel casing styles when viewing in standard grid mode.
- **Queue Clear Update**: The **Clear Queue** action now also cleanly removes the currently playing song when cleared.
- **Download Speed Meter**: Real-time MB/s throughput meter and estimated time remaining during plugin updates and artwork downloads.

#### 📚 Organization & Discovery
- **"Group into One Album"**: Effortlessly consolidate multi-disc releases or disparate folder tracks into a single unified album directly from the album context menu.
- **M3U8 Playlist Export**: Export custom playlists to standard `.m3u8` playlist files with absolute or relative path options.
- **Last.fm Taste Mixes**: "From Your Last.fm" mixes and "Discover on Last.fm" recommendations integrated directly into the Home view.
- **Expanded Localization**: Comprehensive German language translation added, and hard-coded English strings across dialogs and menus replaced with localized resource strings.

---

### 🪟 Windows 7 SP1 & Lossless Audiophile Foundation

All features introduced in our v1.6.7 release remain 100% active and optimized:
- **Bit-Perfect WASAPI Exclusive Mode**: Direct, unadulterated PCM delivery bypassing Windows Audio Engine (`audiodg.exe`) and OS volume sliders.
- **Hardware Sample-Rate Locking**: Instant hardware clock switching across 44.1 kHz, 48 kHz, 88.2 kHz, 96 kHz, 176.4 kHz, and 192 kHz.
- **Dedicated Hardware Stop Button**: Fully releases exclusive DAC locks and COM endpoints immediately, letting other applications produce sound without quitting Noctis. Configurable via **Settings → Player → Player Island Buttons**.
- **Self-Contained .NET 6 Runtime**: Requires zero external runtimes or updates on Windows 7 SP1 x64.
- **Native Aero Glass Titlebar & Win32 DWM Clipping**: Glass borders and smooth 36px rounded popup menus with zero white corner boxes or rendering artifacts.
- **Bundled LibVLC 3.0.21 x64**: Complete offline codecs for FLAC, ALAC, WAV, APE, Opus, AAC, and MP3.

### 📦 Installation

#### Windows 7 SP1 (64-bit) Portable
1. Download `Noctis-v1.6.8-Win7-x64.zip` from Assets below.
2. Extract to your desired folder (e.g. `C:\apps\Noctis` or `D:\apps\Noctis`).
3. Launch `Noctis.exe`. No additional runtimes or frameworks required!

#### Existing Users (In-App Update)
- Launch Noctis v1.6.7 with an internet connection. The in-app updater will detect `v1.6.8`, download the release package, and automatically update your installation.
